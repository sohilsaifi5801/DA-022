# Data Dictionary

This folder contains road accident statistics
published by the Ministry of Road Transport
and Highways (MoRTH).

## Files

- national_trends_2019_2023.csv
- severity_2019_2023.csv
- time_interval_2019_2023.csv
- road_category_2022_2023.csv
- weather_2022_2023.csv
- monthly_2023.csv
- rural_urban_2019_2023.csv

## Data Format

The files contain aggregated government
statistics, not individual accident records.

## Source

MoRTH - Road Accidents in India 2023.

## Limitations

Data coverage varies by year and category.
Accident counts do not directly represent
accident risk.
